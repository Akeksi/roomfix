Speakers

Subwoofer

The subwoofer is on its own output device. roomfix measures the delay between the two devices through the playback engine and compensates it. Leave the Windows volume of both devices and the volume of the amplifiers as they are after measuring; use the Windows volume of the system audio source to listen louder or quieter.

    2 · Measure

    Put the microphone at ear height in the listening position, pointing at the ceiling. Stay quiet during the sweeps.
