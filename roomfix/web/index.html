1 · Setup

Speakers

Subwoofer

The subwoofer is on its own output device. roomfix measures the delay between the two devices through the playback engine and compensates it. Leave the Windows volume of both devices and the volume of the amplifiers as they are after measuring; use the volume keys of the keyboard, or the slider at the top of this page, to listen louder or quieter.

TV App source

For what a TV's own apps play (streaming, antenna), which never passes through the PC. Connect the TV's optical output to the S/PDIF input of a sound card and set the TV's digital audio output to PCM. With the source "TV App" (top of this page, tray icon or phone), roomfix plays it from that input. Press Apply correction to use changes.

The timing between the TV's speakers and the subwoofer cannot be measured in this source: set it by ear above.
